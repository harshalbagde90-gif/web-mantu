RUNWAY FREE: MAKE YOUR FIRST PRODUCT SHOT

Open Runway: https://app.runwayml.com/
Free plan details: https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/50404627334547-Free-plan-details
Credits and model costs: https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/15124877443219-How-do-credits-work
Higgsfield free plan: https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/plans/what-does-the-free-plan-include

1. Sign in and check the credit balance. Runway lists 125 one-time credits on the Free plan. Spent credits do not refresh. Free video exports have a watermark.
2. Open the video generation workspace and choose an image-to-video model available on your account. Models and costs can change. An upgrade prompt means that option is unavailable on your current plan.
3. Upload a product photo you own or can use. For this example, use a coffee tin with its label facing the camera.
4. Paste the prompt below. Pick a short available duration. Read the displayed cost before you click Generate.
5. Play the result. Check the label, product shape and camera motion, then export the clip if it works for your use.

COPYABLE PROMPT
Slow camera push toward the coffee tin. Warm light moves across the label. Keep the tin steady and the label readable.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- Warped label: simplify the motion and try a clearer photo. Check the cost before retrying.
- Upgrade prompt: choose another eligible model on the Free plan.
- Out of credits: the starter balance does not refill. Check alternatives or plan costs before paying.
- Visible watermark: this is expected on the Free plan.
