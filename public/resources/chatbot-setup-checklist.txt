WebMantu: Website Chatbot Setup Checklist

1. Decide what the bot should handle
List the five questions your team answers most often. Start with service information, opening hours and how to request a quote. Define what the bot should hand to a person, including complaints, unusual requests and anything it cannot verify.

2. Prepare the answers
Create a small knowledge base with approved service descriptions, contact details and policies. Give each answer an owner and a review date. If pricing depends on scope, explain how to get a quote rather than guessing.

3. Map the conversation
- Welcome: “What can we help you with?”
- Clarify: Ask one question at a time.
- Answer: Use the approved information and provide a relevant next step.
- Handoff: If unsure, say so and offer your contact form or another staffed channel.

4. Route enquiries
Collect only the details your team needs to respond. Explain who receives them and avoid asking for passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. Test that a real enquiry reaches the right inbox or CRM owner.

5. Test before launch
- Ask a common question, a vague question and a question with no approved answer.
- Try the chat on a small phone and with a keyboard.
- Check that the fallback channel works if the bot fails.
- Check that staff can take over and correct outdated information.

6. Review after launch
Track unanswered questions and successful handoffs. Use them to update the knowledge base. A chat session is not the same as a qualified lead or sale.
