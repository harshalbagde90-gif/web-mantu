GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE PHOTO SHOT LIST

1. Logo. Use your current, legible logo.
2. Cover. Pick one well-lit image that represents your actual business.
3. Exterior. If customers visit, photograph the entrance from the street.
4. Interior or work area. Show the space in daylight and remove clutter.
5. Product or service. Photograph a real product or work in progress. Get permission when people or customer property are recognizable.

EXPORT AND UPLOAD
Use JPG or PNG, 10 KB to 5 MB per image. Google recommends 720 × 720 px and requires at least 250 × 250 px. Keep the subject in focus and avoid heavy filters or significant alterations. Use actual business photos.
Open your verified Business Profile in Google Search or Maps. Choose Photos, then Add photos. Use the dedicated cover and logo controls where available. After upload, inspect the public listing on a phone. Google may display a different photo first.
